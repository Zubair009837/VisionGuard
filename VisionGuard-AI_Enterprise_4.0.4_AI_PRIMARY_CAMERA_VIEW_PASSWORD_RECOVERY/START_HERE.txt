VISIONGUARD AI - HISTORICAL RECORDING FINDER V5 STABLE

1. Extract this ZIP to a simple folder, e.g. C:\VisionGuard-AI-HISTORICAL-RECORDING-V4
2. Double-click RUN_BACKEND.bat and keep its window OPEN.
3. Double-click RUN_FRONTEND.bat and keep its window OPEN.
4. Open the URL printed by Vite (usually http://localhost:5173).
5. Log in, then open Historical Recording Finder.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT type “Uvicorn running on...” into CMD. That is output, not a command.
